# Research-to-action pathway

## Impact tiers

| Tier | Decision purpose | Required evidence | Investment posture |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish credible positioning | Claim tied to a specific artifact, buyer problem, alternative and limitation | Publish precise language; no implementation funding |
| 2 | Establish integration feasibility | Open-standard interface, wallet or facilitator route, stablecoin access, recovery behaviour and measured integration effort | Fund a short technical design or conformance exercise |
| 3 | Establish buyer commitment | External buyers define a paid workflow, acceptance criteria, budget or decision condition and reason to prefer the control | Consider a bounded pilot or implementation grant |
| 4 | Establish adoption | Paid external buyers, repeat production use, multiple providers, completion, refund and dispute outcomes and measurable Cardano-dependent value | Consider scale funding subject to economics and governance |

Movement between tiers requires new evidence. Directory presence, announcements or transaction counts do not substitute for the next tier.

## Recommended engagement 1

### Buyer-backed escrowed service design

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Objective | Determine whether conditional settlement changes a purchase decision for one machine-delivered service |
| Relevant owners | CPC or Cardano Foundation sponsor; Masumi or Sokosumi; two independent service providers; at least three external buyers |
| Dependency | A service with measurable non-delivery, refund or dispute risk and an operational wallet or stablecoin route |
| Evidence threshold | Written workflow, acceptance criteria, price, buyer reason for using escrow and comparison with simple payment or conventional marketplace escrow |
| Impact tier | Tier 3 if buyer commitment is real; otherwise remain at Tier 1 |
| Next decision point | Before funding production implementation |
| Stop condition | Buyers will not pay, switch or accept added friction for conditional settlement |

## Recommended engagement 2

### Open-standard integration and conformance

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Objective | Make Cardano conditional settlement reachable from an existing agent and payment stack |
| Relevant owners | Masumi or Cardano developer team; x402 or MPP facilitator; wallet and stablecoin provider; independent non-Cardano builder |
| Dependency | Stable interfaces, test assets and a defined failure-recovery process |
| Evidence threshold | Reference integration, conformance results, measured implementation time, wallet and settlement support and successful recovery from failed or disputed work |
| Impact tier | Tier 2 |
| Next decision point | After one independent builder completes the integration |
| Stop condition | Cardano integration remains materially harder and no buyer values the difference |

## Recommended engagement 3

### Minimal receipt and assurance design review

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Objective | Test whether a neutral minimal receipt improves audit, risk or dispute handling across organisations |
| Relevant owners | Two independent control owners; enterprise integrator; privacy specialist; Cardano anchoring provider |
| Dependency | Defined authority, action, payment and outcome references and a clear off-chain evidence process |
| Evidence threshold | Accepted receipt schema, privacy assessment, responsible operator, dispute interpretation and comparison with signed private logs or transparency services |
| Impact tier | Tier 2 and Tier 3 only if control owners accept operational use |
| Next decision point | Before technical implementation or a regulated-workflow pilot |
| Stop condition | Existing signed logs or credential systems meet all material requirements at lower cost |

## Measurement rules

The next engagements should report the following measures separately:

- external and internal buyers;
- paid and unpaid tasks;
- unique and repeat buyers;
- completed, refunded and disputed services;
- accepted and rejected outcomes;
- implementation and support effort;
- transaction and settlement costs;
- incremental Cardano transactions and stablecoin use; and
- the buyer or risk outcome that depends on the Cardano component.

The evaluator should not attribute every application interaction to Cardano. Cardano-side value should include only activity or outcomes that depend on the chain's settlement or evidence function.

## Decision rule

The committee should approve a product or pilot grant only if at least one engagement reaches Tier 3 and the integration engagement passes Tier 2. A broad AI campaign should wait until repeat production evidence reaches Tier 4.
