# Quality assurance and AI use

## Contract alignment

| Requirement | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Decision memo | Complete | Final recommendation, conditions, actions, confidence and exclusions |
| Full evidence report | Complete | Method, sources, respondent categories, decision gates, evidence tables, limitations and confidence labels |
| Public summary | Complete | Non-confidential summary within the five-page content limit |
| Cross-RFP handoff memo | Complete | Findings routed to adjacent workstreams with scope boundaries |
| 30-minute readout materials | Complete | Slide sequence, speaker plan and anticipated Q&A |
| Live presentation and community Q&A | Pending external delivery | Date, venue, recording and Q&A record will be added after the session |
| Recorded project overview | Pending external delivery | A 20 to 45 minute recording will use the final readout materials |

## Quality controls

- The final evidence cut contains 75 public and technical source entries and 33 qualifying field records across six agreed stakeholder segments.
- Dynamic Cardano, Masumi and Sokosumi facts were refreshed on 29 October 2026 and remain labelled as provider-reported or platform-reported where applicable.
- Weighted use-case scores were recalculated against the Milestone 1 rubric.
- Capability, observable activity, commercial adoption and strategic inference remain separate.
- Every decision gate includes a conclusion, confidence, action and material limitation.
- Priority recommendations identify the relevant owner, evidence, remaining uncertainty, reversal condition and prohibited inference.
- Negative and no-go findings remain visible.
- Respondent identities, raw notes and organisation-to-response mappings are excluded from the public record.
- Optional proposal add-ons remain outside the completion claim.

## AI-use disclosure

AI tools assisted with source triage, research organisation, comparison structures, consistency checks and drafting. They were not treated as evidence. They did not determine respondent qualification, confidence, scores or recommendations. IEI/TKNFRA selected the sources, assessed credibility, checked claims and approved the submitted text.

## Retention and confidentiality

IEI/TKNFRA retains controlled interview notes, the detailed attribution ledger and supporting research records for at least 12 months. Public materials use anonymised segment-level synthesis unless a source is already public or explicit attribution is permitted.
