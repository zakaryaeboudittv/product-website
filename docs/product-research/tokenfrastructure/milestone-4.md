## Final recommendation

The recommendation is a **conditional go** for a narrow Cardano position in assurance and conditional settlement for open AI-agent services. Cardano should test whether escrow, bounded authority and minimal receipts improve a real purchase, risk or dispute outcome. The evidence does not support a general AI-chain position, machine-payment leadership, a compute or model marketplace, or a broad claim that Cardano makes AI safe.

The next investment decision should follow three tests: independent buyer demand, integration through open payment and authorization standards, and production evidence that the Cardano-based control changes an outcome that buyers value.
